# SUPPLIER & PROVIDER EVIDENCE REGISTER: PRIMARY LEGAL CITATIONS

**Project:** AI Tools Zone (`https://aitoolszone.tech/`)  
**Audit Stage:** Phase 2.2 Provider Terms & Sourcing Verification  
**Scope:** Primary legal citations, vendor policy clauses, and empirical sourcing levels.

## 1. Six-Tier Sourcing Evidence Hierarchy

Every sourcing route in the register is grounded in one of six formal evidence levels:
1. **Level 1 — Official Provider Authorization:** Direct written reseller/distributor contract from the software vendor.
2. **Level 2 — Authorized Distributor Agreement:** Formal sub-distribution contract with an authorized distributor (e.g. Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider).
3. **Level 3 — Verifiable Supplier Documentation:** Supplier provides verifiable corporate registration, business tax ID, and written 30-day warranty agreement.
4. **Level 4 — Supplier Quotation without Authorization Evidence:** Supplier provides wholesale price lists on WhatsApp/Telegram without legal business proof.
5. **Level 5 — Informal Supplier Claim:** Unsubstantiated claims made by account brokers on social media or forums.
6. **Level 6 — Unverified Market Observation:** Merely observing that a competitor sells the product.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Policy:** Sourcing evidence at Level 4, 5, or 6 is **never** promoted to a higher level. Products with Level 4–6 sourcing remain `PENDING SUPPLIER VERIFICATION`.
