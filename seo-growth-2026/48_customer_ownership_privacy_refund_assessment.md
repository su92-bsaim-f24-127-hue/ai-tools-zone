# CUSTOMER ACCOUNT OWNERSHIP, DATA PRIVACY & REFUND ASSESSMENT

**Project:** AI Tools Zone (`https://aitoolszone.tech/`)  
**Audit Stage:** Phase 2.2 Customer Protection & Compliance Assessment

## 1. Account Ownership & Control Matrix

Customer disputes and chargebacks in Pakistan are overwhelmingly caused by ambiguous account ownership. AI Tools Zone establishes four strict delivery tiers:

| Delivery Tier | Technical Mechanism | Customer Ownership | Customer Controls Password & MFA? | Administrator Revocation Power? | Customer Privacy Grade | Approved for Catalog? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Native Personal Invite** | User receives invitation link on personal email (e.g. Microsoft 365 Family, Grammarly Team) | **100% Personal** | YES (Personal password, personal MFA) | Host organizer can remove seat, but CANNOT access customer files | **A+ (100% Private)** | **Approved for Sourcing Test** |
| **Tier 2: Concierge Assisted Checkout** | Facilitator pays vendor invoice directly on user's personal account via virtual card (Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro) | **100% Personal** | YES (Customer owns email & credentials) | NONE (Vendor bills account directly) | **A+ (100% Private)** | **Approved for Sourcing Test** |
| **Tier 3: Dedicated Standalone Account** | Standalone pre-registered account delivered to customer with login credentials (QuillBot, Runway) | **Dedicated** | Customer can change password if email access transferred | Supplier may recover account if email access retained | **B (Private if password changed)** | **Conditional on Sourcing Test** |
| **Tier 4: Shared Multi-User Account** | 3–5 users share a single login credential or browser session (Midjourney, SEMrush, All-in-One) | **Shared (0% Ownership)** | NO (Password managed by supplier) | ANY user or supplier can lock out account | **F (Zero Privacy; Data Leakage)** | **STRICTLY REJECTED** |

## 2. Customer Privacy & Intellectual Property Safeguards

- **Developer Source Code:** Software engineers using Cursor AI, Claude, or Copilot handle confidential proprietary code. Shared accounts expose repository tokens and proprietary algorithms. **Zero shared developer accounts are permitted.**
- **Academic Theses & Documents:** Students proofreading dissertations on Grammarly or QuillBot must have private document folders. Team workspaces must be audited to ensure administrator document viewing is disabled.
- **Personal Cloud Data:** Microsoft 365 Family stores personal photos, tax records, and documents. Microsoft's architecture isolates each family member's 1TB storage completely; the organizer has zero access.

## 3. Transparent Customer Terms & Warranty Obligations

To prevent WhatsApp order friction, customer terms must explicitly state:
1. **Exact Plan Tier:** Clearly distinguish between personal subscriptions and organization member seats.
2. **Usage & Rate Limits:** Claude peak-hour limits, Cursor 500 fast requests, and Runway generation seconds must be stated upfront.
3. **Replacement SLA:** 7-day replacement warranty for standalone accounts; 30-day replacement for annual/team invitations.
4. **Refund Policy:** 100% full refund if an order cannot be activated within 2 hours of payment confirmation.
5. **Prohibited Marketing Claims:** Marketing copy must never claim 'Lifetime access' for subscription software, '100% no-ban guarantee', or 'Unlimited generation' where vendor quotas apply.
