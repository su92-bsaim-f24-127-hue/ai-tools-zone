## 1. Transparent Multi-Factor Prioritization Scoring Model

Products are evaluated across five objective commercial dimensions (100 points maximum):

1. **Documented Commercial Search Demand in Pakistan (25 pts):** Documented search interest, competitor organic ranking volume, and local search queries.
2. **Buyer Purchase Intent & Payment Friction (25 pts):** Severity of credit card decline issues and necessity of local PKR/JazzCash facilitation.
3. **Legitimate & Sourcing Feasibility (20 pts):** Existence of clean, repeatable fulfillment channels (official team invites, virtual card facilitation, personal accounts).
4. **Fulfilment Simplicity & Low Account-Ban Risk (15 pts):** Minimal customer support overhead, zero shared login friction, stable sessions.
5. **Margin & Pricing Sustainability (15 pts):** Room for healthy gross margins (PKR 400–1,500/order) against competitor benchmark prices.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why Competitor Frequency Alone Was Rejected:** Competitors frequently list fragile, high-churn tools (such as shared cookie portals) simply because other resellers list them. Our scoring model weights operational reliability, zero account bans, and customer retention over raw competitor frequency.
