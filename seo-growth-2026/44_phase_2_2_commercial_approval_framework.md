## 1. Executive Purpose & Governance Principles

Phase 2.2 establishes an evidence-based commercial gate for AI Tools Zone. It operates under a strict governing mandate:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Core Commercial Principle:** Competitor listings and high search volume scores are research signals—they are **not** commercial authorization. AI Tools Zone must sell only products it can legitimately source, reliably fulfil, legally protect customers while delivering, and price at sustainable PKR gross margins.

AI Tools Zone explicitly rejects six common industry fallacies:
1. A competitor listing a tool does **not** prove resale authorization.
2. A provider's team or family-sharing feature does **not** authorize commercial marketplace resale.
3. A single successful test upgrade does **not** prove an ongoing, repeatable sourcing route.
4. A high keyword opportunity score does **not** prove business profitability.
5. A low grey-market supplier price does **not** guarantee sustainable profit (chargebacks and refunds quickly turn paper profits into net losses).
6. A product cannot be delivered safely merely because another marketplace advertises it.

## 2. The Five Commercial Feasibility Pillars

Every product candidate must satisfy five rigorous evaluation pillars before reaching the owner's catalog approval desk:

1. **Legal & Terms Authorization:** Does the provider's official Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, or reseller program permit the proposed subscription arrangement? Or does it expose buyers to account termination?
2. **Sourcing Authenticity & Continuity:** Is the wholesale supplier verifiable? Are payment methods legitimate (non-fraudulent cards, non-trial exploits)? Can the supplier guarantee 30-day continuity?
3. **Landed Cost & Gross Margin Viability:** Can AI Tools Zone cover the provider cost, bank FX markup (3.5%–5%), FBR Section 236Y withholding tax (5% filer), local payment fees (1.5%), support costs, and warranty reserves, while offering a competitive PKR price?
4. **Customer Ownership & Data Privacy:** Does the customer control their email, password, and MFA? Can an administrator view the customer's confidential prompts, code, projects, or documents?
5. **Operational Support & Refund Exposure:** If an account is suspended or rate-limited by the vendor, what is AI Tools Zone's financial and customer support replacement liability?

## 3. Seven Standard Commercial Approval Statuses

Every candidate in [`45_individual_product_commercial_approval_matrix.csv`](file:///C:/Users/ELITEBOOK/projects/ai-tools-zone/seo-growth-2026/45_individual_product_commercial_approval_matrix.csv) is assigned exactly one of these seven standardized decision statuses:

| Approval Status | Operational Meaning | Sourcing & Production Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| `APPROVED FOR COMMERCIAL TESTING` | Product possesses a verifiable, low-risk test delivery route (e.g. concierge card checkout on customer email, or native family invitation) | Permitted for controlled single-seat owner test purchase. **NOT authorized for live publication.** |
| `PENDING PROVIDER AUTHORIZATION` | Provider terms prohibit third-party account resale or multi-tenant seat subdivision without written partner agreement | Blocked from testing until an authorized partner or MSP contract is documented. |
| `PENDING SUPPLIER VERIFICATION` | Wholesale supplier claims exist, but business identity, payment validity, or replacement warranty is unverified | Blocked from customer sales until supplier passes test purchase and identity audit. |
| `PENDING LANDED COST` | Wholesale landed cost, FX tax burden, or Pakistani consumer demand volume is unverified | Retained in draft catalog; hold until wholesale rate card and margins are established. |
| `PENDING CUSTOMER POLICY` | Specific operational usage rules (e.g. generation credit limits, device caps) must be drafted | Blocked until customer terms and non-refundable limits are explicitly defined. |
| `REJECTED — TERMS OR COMMERCIAL RISK` | Inherent terms breach, severe ban risk, academic dishonesty, or fraudulent session-injection model | **Permanently rejected.** Prohibited from live catalog. |
| `APPROVED FOR CATALOG PUBLICATION` | Fully verified sourcing, legal compliance, confirmed landed cost, approved pricing, drafted terms, and explicit owner sign-off | Reserved exclusively for final owner sign-off. **Zero products assigned automatically.** |

## 4. Landed Cost & PKR Gross Margin Formula

To prevent operating at a hidden loss, landed cost is calculated using this audited formula:
$$\text{Total Landed Cost (PKR)} = \text{Base Sourcing Cost} + \text{FX Fee (4\%)} + \text{Sec 236Y Tax (5\%)} + \text{Gateway Fee (1.5\%)} + \text{Ops Support} + \text{Warranty Reserve (5\%)}$$
$$\text{Gross Profit (PKR)} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Total Landed Cost}$$
$$\text{Gross Margin (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Selling Price}} \right) \times 100$$
$$\text{Break-Even Price} = \text{Total Landed Cost}$$
$$\text{Minimum Sustainable Price} = \text{Total Landed Cost} \times 1.25 \quad (\text{Targeting } \ge 20\% \text{ net margin})$$
