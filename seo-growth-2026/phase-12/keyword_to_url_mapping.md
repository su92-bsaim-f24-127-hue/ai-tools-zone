### Specific Cannibalization Safeguards
1. **Homepage vs. Master Buying Guide:**
   - Homepage focuses on catalog discovery, search filtering, quick price comparison, and instant ordering.
   - Master Buying Guide (`/buy-ai-tools-in-pakistan/`) provides long-form educational depth explaining account types, renewal cycles, vendor vs. third-party access, and legal compliance.
2. **ChatGPT Plus Product Page vs. Buying Guide vs. Payment Blog:**
   - `/products/chatgpt-plus/` is purely transactional (1-click WhatsApp order).
   - `/how-to-buy-chatgpt-plus-in-pakistan/` is the complete evaluation guide for buyers deciding how and where to purchase.
   - `/blog/how-to-pay-for-chatgpt-plus-pakistan/` remains exclusively focused on resolving Pakistani credit/debit card decline error codes.
3. **Student Root Guide vs. Existing Student Blog Articles:**
   - `/best-ai-tools-for-students-pakistan/` serves as the authoritative, definitive 9-use-case guide.
   - `/blog/best-ai-tools-for-students-pakistan/` links directly to `/best-ai-tools-for-students-pakistan/` as the primary reference guide.
   - `/blog/ai-tools-for-pakistani-students-research-thesis/` remains focused specifically on thesis writing and literature reviews.
4. **Video & Image Guides:**
   - `/ai-video-editing-tools-pakistan/` focuses on post-production editing workflows (Descript, CapCut, Runway).
   - `/blog/best-ai-video-generators-pakistan/` focuses on text-to-video generative AI models (Veo, Runway).
   - `/ai-image-generator-subscriptions-pakistan/` focuses on commercial subscription licensing, credit packs, and buying methods.
   - `/blog/best-ai-image-generators-pakistan/` focuses on creative art styles and prompt comparisons.
