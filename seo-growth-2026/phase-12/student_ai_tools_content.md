## 4. Academic Integrity & Ethical Guidelines

Pakistani universities (including NUST, LUMS, FAST-NUCES, UET, Punjab University, COMSATS, and IBA) adhere to Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan guidelines on plagiarism and academic ethics:

1. **AI as a Tutor, Not a Ghostwriter:** Use AI tools to explain concepts, suggest structural outlines, and proofread your original writing. Never ask an AI model to write your complete assignment or thesis dissertation.
2. **Always Independently Verify Citations:** Large language models can occasionally hallucinate references. Never paste a citation into your bibliography without manually confirming that the paper exists in Google Scholar or a library database.
3. **Transparent Disclosure:** If your department requires it, include an AI statement: "Artificial intelligence (ChatGPT / Grammarly) was utilized solely for grammar refinement and literature discovery; all analysis and conclusions are original work by the author."
4. **Data Privacy:** Never upload unpublished experimental lab data, confidential clinical patient notes, or proprietary intellectual property into shared or public chatbots.
