# PRIORITIZED 90-DAY GROWTH ROADMAP: CATALOG EXPANSION & SEO SCALING

**Target Platform:** AI Tools Zone (`https://aitoolszone.tech/`)  
**Core Objective:** Capture Pakistan organic search demand, establish dominance across AI tool keywords, and maximize WhatsApp order conversions.

## 1. Roadmap Architecture Overview

The 90-day plan is structured into four sequential, risk-controlled phases:

### Phase 1: Days 1–15 — Commercial Validation & Top 10 Flagship Sourcing
- **Milestone 1.1:** Owner review of `missing_products_for_aitoolszone.csv` and approval of commercial selling prices for Top 10 Tier 1 tools:
  1. Claude Pro (3.5 Sonnet)
  2. Cursor AI Pro
  3. Midjourney Pro
  4. Perplexity Pro
  5. Grammarly Premium
  6. QuillBot Premium
  7. SEMrush Pro
  8. GitHub Copilot
  9. Runway ML Gen-3
  10. Coursera Plus
- **Milestone 1.2:** Finalize wholesale supplier agreements and confirm delivery SLAs (15–30 min) and replacement warranty terms (7 to 30 days).
- **Milestone 1.3:** Prepare brand logos in `assets/product-logos/` for the approved tools.

### Phase 2: Days 16–30 — Controlled Deployment of Batch 1 & SERP Indexation
- **Milestone 2.1:** Move approved Batch 1 products from `draft-catalog-recommendations.json` into `data/catalog.json`.
- **Milestone 2.2:** Execute `build_site.py` to compile static HTML pages, update `sitemap.xml`, and generate Product/FAQ JSON-LD schemas.
- **Milestone 2.3:** Perform staging verification and request explicit user authorization for merge and push.
- **Milestone 2.4:** Submit new canonical URLs via Google Search Console URL inspection and IndexNow.

### Phase 3: Days 31–60 — Batch 2 Rollout (17 Remaining Tier 1 Tools) & Comparison Hubs

  - `/compare/claude-vs-chatgpt-plus-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/midjourney-vs-leonardo-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/grammarly-vs-quillbot-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/perplexity-vs-chatgpt-search-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/runway-vs-capcut-pro-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/heygen-vs-elevenlabs-pakistan/`
  - `/compare/windsurf-vs-cursor-pakistan/`

### Phase 4: Days 61–90 — Long-Tail Optimization, Conversion Tuning & Agency B2B
- **Milestone 4.1:** Onboard Tier 2 specialized developer and agency tools (Supabase, Railway, Firecrawl, Make/Integromat, Ahrefs Starter).
