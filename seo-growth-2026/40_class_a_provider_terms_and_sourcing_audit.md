## 2. Critical Distinctions in Account Fulfilment

1. **Invitation to User's Own Email (Gold Standard):** Microsoft 365 Family, Grammarly Team, GitHub Copilot. The buyer never shares passwords; they accept an invitation link on their existing personal account. Zero credential risk.

2. **Concierge Checkout / Card Facilitation:** Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, Cursor AI Pro. The buyer provides their email, and AI Tools Zone pays the vendor invoice via international virtual card rails. The buyer maintains total account ownership.

3. **Dedicated Private Account Handover:** Midjourney Pro, Runway ML, QuillBot. Sourced as independent standalone accounts with single-user credentials handed to the customer. Requires 7-to-30 day replacement coverage.

4. **Shared Multi-User Accounts:** **STRICTLY PROHIBITED.** High chargeback rate, severe privacy violations, and immediate platform bans.
