## 2. In-Depth Profiles for Approved Test Candidates

### 1. Microsoft Office 365 (1TB) — `APPROVED FOR COMMERCIAL TESTING`
- **Sourcing Mechanism:** Owner purchases 1 official Microsoft 365 Family subscription ($99.99/yr, ~PKR 28,200/yr). Generates 5 official invitation links.
- **Landed Cost:** PKR 28,200 / 6 = PKR 4,700 base + 4% FX + 5% tax = **PKR 4,950 / user / year**.
- **Competitor Benchmark:** AIToolGems advertises PKR 2,200/yr (likely using subsidized regional tenants or cracked enterprise keys).
- **Commercial Strategy:** Position AI Tools Zone as the **100% Genuine Personal Invitation** provider. Emphasize that customer links their own `@outlook.com` or `@gmail.com` with zero password sharing and zero admin file access.
- **Owner Action:** Authorize purchase of 1 annual Family subscription to test invite delivery and establish retail price.

### 2. Claude Pro (3.5 Sonnet) — `APPROVED FOR COMMERCIAL TESTING`
- **Sourcing Mechanism:** Assisted payment concierge. Customer registers their personal Claude account; AI Tools Zone processes the $20 USD Stripe checkout using a funded virtual card (SadaPay/NayaPay).
- **Landed Cost:** $20.00 * 282 = PKR 5,640 base + PKR 225.60 FX (4%) + PKR 282.00 Sec 236Y (5%) = **PKR 6,147.60**.
- **Competitor Benchmark:** Competitors advertise PKR 2,200 (shared accounts) and PKR 5,500 (private).
- **Commercial Strategy:** Target high-earning software houses and Upwork/Fiverr developers who require 100% private data privacy and refuse to share sessions. Price at **PKR 6,800–7,200/mo** as a premium white-glove assisted payment service.
- **Owner Action:** Test concierge checkout on 3 pilot developer orders.
