# MASTER KEYWORD MAPPING & EDITORIAL CONTENT ARCHITECTURE PLAN

**Subject:** Information Architecture, Canonical Mapping & Internal Link Topology  
**Goal:** Maximize Organic Pakistan Search Traffic and Prevent Keyword Cannibalization

## 1. Information Architecture & Cannibalization Safeguards

To avoid internal keyword competition between product pages, category hubs, and blog guides, we establish strict query routing boundaries:

1. **Transactional & Price Queries (`buy [tool]`, `[tool] price pakistan`):**  
   - Mapped exclusively to `/products/[tool-slug]/` product landing pages.  
   - Primary H1: `[Tool Name] in Pakistan | PKR Price & Instant Activation`.  
2. **Category Commercial Investigation (`best ai tools for [category] pakistan`):**  
   - Mapped exclusively to `/categories/[category-slug]/` index pages.  
3. **Head-to-Head Comparison Queries (`[tool a] vs [tool b] pakistan`):**  
   - Mapped exclusively to `/compare/[tool-a]-vs-[tool-b]-pakistan/` comparison hubs.  
4. **Payment Troubleshooting & Technical How-To (`how to pay [tool] without credit card`):**  
   - Mapped exclusively to `/blog/[slug]/` editorial guides.

## 2. Hub-and-Spoke Internal Linking Topology

Every new product page integrates into AI Tools Zone's semantic hub-and-spoke network:

- **Category Parent Hub:** Links down to all member products with live PKR pricing badges.
- **Product Pages:** Link up to Category Parent Hub, laterally to 4 Related Tools in the same workflow, and forward to relevant comparison pages.
- **Blog Guides:** Link contextually to featured product landing pages with clear callout boxes.
- **FAQ Hub (`/faq/`):** Links directly to product order anchors and payment guides.
