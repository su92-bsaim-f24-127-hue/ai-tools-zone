# AI Tools Zone earned backlink and authority plan

**Researched:** 3 November 2026 (Asia/Karachi)
**Status:** Prospect research only. No person or organization has been contacted, and no external content has been published.

## Link-worthy material to prepare

The most credible basis for a citation is a genuinely useful resource that stands without a purchase link. Prioritize:

1. A dated Pakistan-focused guide to evaluating digital subscription offers: account ownership, access type, eligibility, limits, payment route, cancellation, and what a third-party seller can and cannot verify.
2. A transparent methodology page describing how listed PKR prices, durations, and account types are collected and updated. Clearly distinguish the site's seller-provided catalog from market-wide research.
3. A practical workshop or handout for students/freelancers on evaluating AI tools for a task, privacy and account sharing, and checking current plan limits against official vendor documentation.

Use original data only when the collection method, date, scope, and source records are real and reproducible. Do not imply a vendor relationship, official reseller status, independently verified availability, or lowest-price claim without evidence. Disclose the owner's commercial interest in any pitch.

## Suggested sequence

1. Keep the access and methodology guides accurate, sourced, dated, and useful without a purchase.
2. Prepare one standalone workshop/handout and a fact-checked outline; disclose the site's interest and the author's role.
3. If the owner decides to proceed, contact one suitable editor/community using its published route and a tailored, non-promotional proposal.
4. Respect editorial control. A mention without a link, referral audience, feedback, or future collaboration can still be useful.
5. Measure earned referring pages in Search Console/Bing and relevant referral engagement. Do not use domain-authority scores as if they were Google ranking measures.

## Link-scheme rules

Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, use automated directory/comment drops, create fake reviews, require keyword-rich anchor text, or pay for followed guest posts. For a genuine sponsorship bought for audience exposure, disclose it and ensure the publisher qualifies its link appropriately. Do not contact anyone or publish external content without the owner's explicit authorization.
