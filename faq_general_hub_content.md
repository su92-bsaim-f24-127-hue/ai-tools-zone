#### Q6: Why do local Pakistani debit cards get declined on official AI platforms?
Most Pakistani commercial bank cards decline international software subscriptions (such as OpenAI, Adobe, Midjourney, or Claude) due to State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) foreign exchange regulations, mandatory 3D-Secure OTP mismatches on recurring subscriptions, international transaction withholding taxes (often adding 10%–16% extra charges), or default international eCommerce restrictions on standard debit cards. AI Tools Zone solves this payment friction by accepting domestic PKR payments without international card requirements. Read our [ChatGPT Plus Pakistan payment guide](/blog/how-to-pay-for-chatgpt-plus-pakistan/).

Simply inform our representative on WhatsApp that you prefer SadaPay or NayaPay, and we will send the matching Raast ID or IBFT account number.

#### Q8: Are there any hidden conversion fees, sales taxes, or service charges?
No. All prices listed on our [PKR pricing page](/pricing/) and individual product cards are transparent and flat. ### Category D: Account Access & Security

#### Q13: What is the difference between Private, Shared, and Invitation access?
- **Private:** You receive dedicated account credentials used exclusively by you. Ideal for confidential client work, private coding projects, and sensitive data.
- **Invitation:** An official invite is sent to your email to join a managed workspace. You use your own login and keep your work private while accessing Pro tier features.
- **Shared:** Multiple verified users share access to a single master account with dedicated profiles (like [Netflix Premium 4K](/products/netflix-premium-4k/) or [Surfshark VPN](/products/surfshark-vpn/)). It provides significant cost savings for budget-conscious users.  
Review our detailed [Private vs Shared Access Guide](/guides/private-vs-shared-access/) before purchasing.

#### Q14: Can other users see my private documents, chats, or projects on a Shared plan?
On shared accounts with individual user profiles (like streaming or VPN services), profiles are distinct. However, for AI chat assistants ([ChatGPT Plus](/products/chatgpt-plus/) or [Gemini Pro](/products/gemini-pro/)), AI Tools Zone lists **Private** access so that your prompt history, uploaded PDF files, and client deliverables remain strictly private to you. We strongly recommend Private plans for all commercial and client deliverables.

#### Q15: How can I avoid account lockouts or bans on shared subscriptions?
To ensure uninterrupted service on shared plans:
1. Never change the master account email, password, or billing settings.
2. Only stream or log in on your assigned screen/profile.
3. Avoid logging into an excessive number of simultaneous devices.  
Learn more in our guide on [avoiding account bans on shared digital subscriptions](/blog/avoiding-account-bans-shared-ai-tools-pakistan/).

#### Q16: Does AI Tools Zone support two-factor authentication (2FA)?
On dedicated **Private** subscriptions (such as private developer or design accounts), you can enable your own authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) or SMS verification to secure your login credentials completely.
