## 1. Keyword Strategy Overview

To capture high-intent Pakistani search demand without triggering keyword stuffing or duplicate content penalties, every product page is mapped to a dedicated primary keyword, 3–5 high-relevance secondary keywords, and 3 localized long-tail search queries.

### Strategic Intent Framework:
1. **Transactional (Buy/Order):** Captures visitors ready to buy immediately who need a trustworthy local payment channel (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast).
2. **Commercial Investigation (Price/Features):** Captures comparison shoppers evaluating whether the PKR price and plan access meet their requirements.
3. **Informational (How-To/Compatibility):** Resolves edge cases around device support, regional availability in Pakistan, and renewal procedures.
