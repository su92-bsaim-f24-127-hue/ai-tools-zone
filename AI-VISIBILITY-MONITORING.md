# Measure discovery through to customers

Updated 22 October 2026. Use webmaster reports as evidence about search impressions and visits; they do not reveal completed WhatsApp orders.

Start with evidence, not forecasts. Current traffic, ranking, citations, WhatsApp inquiries and completed orders are unknown to this agent. Cloudflare's public beacon ID does not grant access to the private dashboard.

## Weekly owner record

Keep a private spreadsheet with the date window, visits, page views, top landing pages, referrers, Search Console impressions/clicks/CTR, Generative AI report impressions and pages where shown, Bing clicks/AI citations where available, WhatsApp inquiries, qualified inquiries, completed orders and cancellations. Do not commit names, phone numbers, transcripts, payments or this private sheet to GitHub. Ask a customer how they found the store only where useful; do not infer an order from a click.

Funnel: search impression → landing page → relevant plan/comparison → WhatsApp click → conversation → confirmed order. A click is only an intent signal; conversation and purchase happen off-site and need seller records. No custom click collector is currently configured. Therefore a website click-to-order conversion rate is not yet measurable end to end.

Cloudflare Web Analytics already records visits/page views privately to the owner's account. The screenshot supplied on 6 October showed **Last 24 hours** (not 7 days), 9 visits and 9 page views. Its listed `/admin` and `/admin/` URLs return 404, so they are requests for missing pages rather than real storefront sections. The generated 404 previously loaded the beacon; this release removes analytics from 404 responses to keep missing-route probes out of storefront visit totals. Recheck the same date window after deployment; the reported count may fall even though qualified visits are unchanged. Its [FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/) says custom events and UTM reporting are not supported; ad blockers and delivery failures also affect measurement. The `aitz:measure` events are bounded integration hooks. They dispatch a CustomEvent locally and conditionally forward to `window.gtag` / `window.dataLayer` if present, without hardcoded credentials. To measure clicks centrally, connect your Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID or collector. Never expose a private dashboard credential in client code.

Available bounded hooks: product_view, category_view, comparison_view, alternative_view, guide_view, pricing_view, tool_search, filter_use, compare_add, tool_finder_start, tool_finder_complete, whatsapp_click, product_whatsapp_click and outbound_vendor_click. Payloads contain only an allowlisted event name, canonical page path, section and optional allowlisted catalog product ID. Search text, query parameters, message bodies, phone numbers and purchase details are excluded.

## Current Google visibility controls

A source review found no nosnippet, max-snippet:0 or data-nosnippet directive excluding page text from Google snippets or AI-feature previews. Googlebot is allowed by the site's public wildcard robots rule. This only establishes that the checked source does not apply those exclusions; it does not establish indexing, inclusion in an AI answer or ranking. The Search Console account-level Search generative AI control cannot be inspected from this workspace and remains unverified. After verification, check **Settings → Search generative AI** and confirm inclusion is selected or inherited from an included parent property. Google's control guide says inclusion is the default but a property can be set to exclude or inherit another property's setting; see [the official control instructions](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024).

Google's Search Console Generative AI performance report has rolled out worldwide; Google says it may not appear when a property has too few impressions. Inspect it after property verification and when report data appears. Keep its impressions/pages separate from standard Search clicks and WhatsApp inquiries. Google also says its AI search features follow normal SEO eligibility and do not require special AI text files or schema. See [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and the [Search Console report help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139).

## AI answer checks

After the owner verifies Search Console, check its Generative AI performance report under Performance. Google announced worldwide availability for all websites from 31 August 2026. The report can show impressions and pages for Search and Discover generative features, countries, and dates; device breakdown is available for Search. For Search, this includes AI Overviews and AI Mode. If the report or data is absent, treat that as unavailable/insufficient report data, not proof of zero visibility. Compare its impression data with conventional Search clicks separately; neither report records an off-site WhatsApp conversation or sale. [Google's report announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports?facet3=pdf) documents the report dimensions.

Monthly, use a fixed small set of questions about Pakistan offers, access and comparisons. Record date, engine/interface, locale, exact question, whether a citation actually appeared and its destination. Compare the same questions; a single personalized response is not a rank or market share. Do not automate prompts to manipulate engines or claim an answer was cited without saving evidence privately. Google's AI Search guidance says normal technical eligibility and helpful, reliable content remain the basis; it specifies no special AI markup or machine-readable file, and indexing or inclusion is not guaranteed ([Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).

## Customer acquisition priorities

First week: verify search properties, check actual offer eligibility and availability, reply consistently to inbound inquiries, and record objections. Weeks 2–3: prioritize the three landing pages with real impressions or buyer questions; improve the answer/offer clarity using those observations. Week 4: compare equal windows and investigate drop-offs. If relevant visits occur without conversations, review price/access uncertainty and support response; if conversations occur without orders, investigate offer fit, entitlement and payment confidence. No artificial testimonials, fabricated urgency, bought backlinks or automatic unsolicited outreach.
