# Semantic Topic Clustering & Content Architecture: aitoolszone.tech

**Methodology:** SERP-Overlap Clustering & Hub-and-Spoke Semantic Architecture (via `seo-cluster` skill)  
**Clustering Rule:** Keywords sharing 4–6 top-10 search results are grouped under the same cluster; keywords sharing 7+ are merged into a single page to prevent keyword cannibalization; keywords sharing 2–3 results are connected via contextual bidirectional cross-links.  
**Domain Strategy:** Hub-and-Spoke Topical Authority Network with 1 Pillar Hub, 6 Core Topic Clusters, 20 Product Spokes, 10 Comparison Spokes, and 16 Supporting Commercial Guides.

## 8. Anti-Cannibalization Matrix & Canonical Deduplication Rules

To eliminate the risk of keyword cannibalization (two pages competing for the same SERP slot):

1. **Transactional Product Queries (`buy X in pakistan`, `X price in pakistan`):**
   * Exclusively routed to the dedicated product landing page (`/products/{slug}/`).
   * No blog post is permitted to target exact-match `buy {product}` as its H1 or primary title.
2. **Comparison Queries (`X vs Y in pakistan`):**
   * Exclusively routed to `/compare/{x}-vs-{y}/`.
   * Both product pages must link to the comparison page in their "Explore more tools" section, but the product page titles must remain strictly product-focused.
3. **How-To & Problem-Solving Queries (`how to pay for X in pakistan`):**
   * Exclusively routed to `/blog/how-to-pay-for-{slug}-pakistan/`.
   * These informational articles contain explicit CTA blocks pointing visitors to the transactional product page.
4. **General Category Queries (`ai video tools in pakistan`):**
   * Exclusively routed to `/categories/{category-slug}/`. Individual products must point their breadcrumb second tier to this category hub.
