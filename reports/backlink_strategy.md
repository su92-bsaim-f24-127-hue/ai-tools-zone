## 7. Backlink Quality Control & Anti-Spam Safeguards

To safeguard the domain from algorithmic penalties (Google Penguin / SpamBrain):
* **No Toxic Link Farms:** Never buy links on Fiverr or bulk PBN networks.
* **Anchor Text Diversity:** Maintain natural anchor distributions:
  - Brand Anchors (45%): `AI Tools Zone`, `aitoolszone.tech`
  - URL / Naked Anchors (25%): `https://aitoolszone.tech/`, `aitoolszone.tech/products/`
  - Topical / Partial Match (20%): `AI tools in Pakistan`, `buy AI subscriptions Pakistan`, `CapCut Pro Pakistan price`
  - Exact Match (10% max): `buy chatgpt plus in pakistan`
* **Quarterly Disavow Review:** Monitor Google Search Console incoming link reports; immediately disavow scraper domains or negative SEO spam.
