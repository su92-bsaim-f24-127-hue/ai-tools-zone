## 5. Content Optimization Checklist for Maximum Search Ranking

Every new article published on `aitoolszone.tech` must satisfy this 10-point checklist:

1. **Exact-Match Local Intent in Title:** Contains PKR price or "Pakistan" in the main title.
2. **Featured Snippet Direct Answer:** A 45–60 word concise summary paragraph directly below the H1 answering the core question.
3. **Structured Comparison Table:** At least one comparative table contrasting prices, features, or duration.
4. **Interactive Local Payment Hook:** Explicit mention of JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Pakistani domestic bank transfers.
5. **Bidirectional Internal Links:** Minimum 3 contextual links to product purchase pages and 2 links to category hubs.
6. **Mobile-Responsive Typography:** Formatted with clean bullet points, bold key takeaways, and scannable H2/H3 headers.
7. **Zero Fluff & High Word Efficiency:** 1,200–1,800 words of actionable substance without repetitive filler.
8. **Schema Validation:** Clean `Article`, `WebPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD markup.
9. **Visual Asset Optimization:** Compressed WebP images with descriptive `alt` tags and explicit `width`/`height` attributes.
10. **Clear Dual WhatsApp CTA:** One mid-article sticky banner and one closing high-contrast button.
