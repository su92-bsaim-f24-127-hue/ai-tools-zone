Academic Integrity, HEC Ethics & Plagiarism Avoidance

Pakistani universities and the Higher Education Commission (HEC) strictly penalize academic dishonesty. Students must understand the ethical boundary when using artificial intelligence:

Use AI as an Intelligent Tutor: Use AI to brainstorm thesis outlines, explain confusing textbook paragraphs, and debug code. Never instruct an AI model to write your entire research paper, essay, or assignment.

Always Independently Verify Citations: LLMs can occasionally hallucinate references. Never paste a citation into your bibliography without manually confirming that the paper exists in Google Scholar or a recognized journal index.

Declare AI Assistance Transparently: Where required by your department, disclose AI tool usage: 'AI assistance (Grammarly / Perplexity) was utilized strictly for grammatical polish and literature discovery; all conclusions and data analysis are original to the author.'
