## 2. Mandatory Quality Criteria & Standards

Every Group A landing page was inspected against the following six non-negotiable consumer-trust standards:

1. **Softened Delivery SLA:** Rigid timeframes (e.g. "15 to 30 minutes after confirmation") must be replaced with `"Confirmed on enquiry (Subject to availability)"`.
2. **Softened Warranty Terms:** Blanket claims (e.g. "365-Day Unconditional Replacement SLA") must be softened to `"Support subject to confirmed terms"`.
3. **Advance Payment Disclosure:** Offer panels must explicitly disclose: `"No advance payment is collected before fulfilment feasibility is confirmed."`
4. **Transparent Lead-Capture CTA:** Buttons must clearly state `"Enquire on WhatsApp"` with proper WhatsApp tracking hooks (`data-wa-product="..."`) and zero misleading automated checkout mechanisms.
5. **Authentic Licensing & Account Architecture:** Disclose actual subscription mechanism (Personal Customer Account, Direct Email Invite, Developer Workspace, Concierge Card Processing) and strictly prohibit deceptive "cheap shared account" or "cookie injection" claims.
6. **Independent Marketplace Disclaimer:** Prominently display: `"AI Tools Zone is an independent digital marketplace. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners."`

## 4. Key Highlights & Consumer Protection Guarantees

1. **Advance Payment Prevention:** Pakistani consumers are frequently defrauded by unverified social media resellers demanding upfront bank transfers for tools they cannot activate. Every AI Tools Zone candidate page clearly warns:
   > *"Confirm current availability, plan limits, and PKR pricing on WhatsApp. No advance payment is collected before fulfilment feasibility is confirmed."*
2. **Clear Distinction between Informational Enquiry and E-Commerce Purchase:** None of the 12 pages feature shopping carts, simulated credit card capture, or automatic order generation. All conversion pathways route to WhatsApp concierge consultation.
3. **EULA & Trademark Protection:** Every page clearly carries the prominent statutory notice:
   > *"AI Tools Zone is an independent digital marketplace. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners."*
