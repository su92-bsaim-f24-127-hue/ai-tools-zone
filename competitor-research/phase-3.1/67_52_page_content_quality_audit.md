## 3. Template Repetition and Differentiation Analysis

While all 52 pages exceed word count standards and contain product-specific feature lists, the audit identified areas where structural boilerplate should be enhanced prior to production indexing:
1. **Payment FAQs:** Questions 5 and 6 on payment methods (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast) and enquiry flow share identical phrasing across all candidate pages. *Recommendation: Vary the context to highlight product-specific delivery timescales (e.g. instant license key delivery vs manual invite processing).*
2. **Benefits Section:** The 'Why buy local' paragraph relies on standard themes (credit card restrictions, withholding taxes). *Recommendation: Incorporate category-specific advantages (e.g. local team onboarding for Cursor/Windsurf; educational billing support for Coursera/Quizlet).*
