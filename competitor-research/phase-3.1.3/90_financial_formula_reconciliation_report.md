## 2. Complete Mathematical Formula Derivations

### Formula 1: Base Currency Conversion
$$\text{Base SBP (PKR)} = \text{Official Retail (USD)} \times \text{SBP Customer Selling Rate}$$
- **SBP Customer Selling Rate:** PKR 276.76 / USD (Documented Nov 2026).

### Formula 2: Bank Foreign Exchange Spread & Federal Excise Duty (FED)
$$\text{Bank Spread Amount} = \text{Base SBP (PKR)} \times 4.00\%$$
$$\text{FED Amount} = \text{Bank Spread Amount} \times 16.00\% = \text{Base SBP (PKR)} \times 0.64\%$$
$$\text{Total Bank Transaction Fee} = \text{Base SBP (PKR)} \times 4.64\%$$
- **Statutory Basis:** Standard Pakistani commercial bank card tariffs (Meezan Bank, HBL, Standard Chartered). 16% FED/PST applied under provincial sales tax on banking services.

### Formula 3: Gross Remittance Outflow
$$\text{Gross Remittance Outflow} = \text{Base SBP (PKR)} + \text{Total Bank Transaction Fee}$$

### Formula 4: Section 236Y Advance Withholding Tax
$$\text{Section 236Y Tax (ATL)} = \text{Gross Remittance Outflow} \times 0.50\%$$
$$\text{Section 236Y Tax (Non-ATL)} = \text{Gross Remittance Outflow} \times 1.00\%$$
- **Official Source:** [FBR Withholding Taxes Rate Card for Tax Year 2027](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/withholding-taxes-rate-card/174298/174301).
- **Correction:** The obsolete 5.0% ATL rate from prior years is completely purged. Section 236Y rate is 0.5% for ATL filers.

### Formula 5: Cash Outlay vs Economic Cost
$$\text{Total Cash Outlay} = \text{Gross Remittance Outflow} + \text{Section 236Y Tax}$$
$$\text{Economic Landed Cost} = \text{Total Cash Outlay} - \text{Adjustable Tax Credit} = \text{Gross Remittance Outflow}$$
- **Legal Proof:** Under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, advance tax collected under Section 236Y is an **adjustable advance tax** creditable against the taxpayer's final annual income tax liability. For an active tax filer, it does not represent an unrecoverable direct expense (COGS).

### Formula 6: Margins and Operating Overhead
$$\text{Gross Contribution (Economic)} = \text{Proposed Selling Price} - \text{Economic Landed Cost}$$
$$\text{Gross Contribution Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Contribution (Economic)}}{\text{Proposed Selling Price}} \times 100$$
$$\text{Total Documented OPEX} = \text{Delivery Labor (PKR 150)} + \text{Support Labor (PKR 200)} + \text{Warranty Reserve (5\% Base)}$$
$$\text{Net Contribution} = \text{Gross Contribution (Economic)} - \text{Total Documented OPEX}$$
$$\text{Net Contribution Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Net Contribution}}{\text{Proposed Selling Price}} \times 100$$
