What 'cheap' really means: Private vs Shared vs Invitation access

Comparing AI subscription prices purely on Pakistani Rupee cost is a common mistake. A PKR 500 offer that stops working after three days or exposes your proprietary client files to strangers is far more expensive than a PKR 2,000 private plan with guaranteed uptime and a replacement warranty.

In Pakistan, digital subscriptions are supplied across four distinct access models: Private accounts, Team Invitations, Shared profiles, and License Keys. Each tier carries a different cost structure, privacy boundary, and technical stability.

  • Private Access (Highest security): You receive dedicated account credentials or activation directly on your email. Your prompt history, custom GPTs, and uploaded documents remain 100% private. Best for professional client work.
  • Team / Workspace Invitations (Best value): The seller sends an invite link to your existing personal email address (common for Canva Pro and Google Workspace). You retain your own login password, while gaining pro workspace features.
  • Shared Logins (Lowest price, strict rules): Multiple users share a pooled account with designated profile pins (common for streaming and bulk editing). Requires strict browser discipline and avoiding simultaneous multiple-device logins to prevent automated vendor flags.
  • License Keys: Permanent or term-based software activation keys (such as Windows 11 Pro) activated directly within the operating system.

The hidden dangers of unverified 'too cheap' sellers

Pakistani social media groups on Facebook, WhatsApp, and Telegram frequently advertise unrealistic offers such as 'ChatGPT Plus Lifetime for Rs. 500' or 'Midjourney unlimited for Rs. 300'. Understanding how these illicit offers work protects buyers from financial loss and data compromise.

Most unrealistic 'lifetime' deals rely on stolen credit-card testing bins, educational trials created with fraudulent credentials, or temporary free tiers. When the payment gateway inevitably reverses the charge or detects invalid billing details, the account is permanently banned, and the anonymous seller vanishes.

Furthermore, using shared accounts with unvetted parties risks prompt leaks. If you upload sensitive business financial statements, academic research drafts, or private client code to an unsecured public shared account, anyone sharing the login can view and download your workspace history.

  • Never trust 'Lifetime' claims on monthly subscription services like ChatGPT Plus, Midjourney, or ElevenLabs; official vendors only operate on monthly or annual billing.
  • Avoid sellers who refuse to specify whether the account is private, shared, or invitation-based before collecting payment.
  • Avoid vendors without a clear replacement warranty policy or public pricing catalog.
  • Never share your personal email password with any seller; reputable digital marketplaces never require your private credentials.
