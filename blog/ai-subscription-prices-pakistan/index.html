Understanding Access Types Before Buying

When comparing prices, the access mechanism directly determines your usage experience and longevity. Confirm which model fits your workflow:

Private Account: You receive dedicated credentials or activation on an isolated account. Best for confidentiality, sensitive client projects, and undisturbed history.

Invitation Access: Your personal email address is invited as a member of an enterprise or educational team. Ideal for collaborative tools like Canva or Notion.

Shared Profile: A single account shared among multiple verified users under strict etiquette rules. Offers maximum affordability for budget-constrained users.

License Key: A genuine alphanumeric digital product key for standalone desktop suites like Microsoft Office or Windows 11 Pro.
