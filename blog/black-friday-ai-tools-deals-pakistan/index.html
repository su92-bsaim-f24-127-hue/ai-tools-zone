Why do Pakistani bank cards fail during international Black Friday sales?

The same three barriers as the rest of the year, amplified by sale-week traffic:

  • International transactions are off or limited by default: many local debit cards need international and online transactions explicitly enabled, and recurring foreign subscriptions are frequently declined even then.
  • Bank foreign-exchange margins and fees: your bank converts at its own rate, not the interbank 276.56, and adds foreign-transaction charges, so the final debit is higher than the converted dollar price.
  • FBR Section 236Y advance tax: for Tax Year 2027 (from 1 July 2026) the FBR rate is 0.5% if you are on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% if you are not, and it is adjustable against your income tax when you file. Ignore guides quoting 10% or 16% — those figures are invented; even last year's rate was 5% / 10%.
