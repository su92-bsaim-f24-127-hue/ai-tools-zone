Google AI Plus Free for Pakistani Students: How to Claim It and When to Pay Instead

Google AI Plus is free for 12 months for eligible college and university students in Pakistan — a plan that normally costs about Rs 1,400 per month, roughly Rs 16,800 for the year — if you claim it on Google's official student page by 31 December 2026. This guide covers who qualifies, how to claim it safely, the auto-renewal catch (it converts to about Rs 1,400/month unless you cancel), and when a paid PKR plan is still the better buy: AI Tools Zone lists Gemini Pro at PKR 2,000 for a full year and ChatGPT Plus at PKR 3,000/month.

Published 18 November 2026 · Updated 23 November 2026 · Research and planning guide

The offer in one paragraph: Google AI Plus free for Pakistani students

Google AI Plus free for Pakistani students is a real Google promotion, not a reseller trick: eligible college and university students in Pakistan can claim 12 months of Google AI Plus at no cost, a plan that normally costs about Rs 1,400 per month — roughly Rs 16,800 for the year. The only official claim page is Google's student page, and the offer must be redeemed by 31 December 2026. The 12 free months run from the day you claim, so claiming on the last day still keeps the plan free until December 2027.

One naming trap first: the free year of Google AI Pro you may have seen advertised is for students in the United States only. Pakistan is one of the 140-plus countries where the free student plan is Google AI Plus instead — a lower tier than Pro, but still a clear step up from a free Gemini account. This guide was published on 18 November 2026, while the offer is open; if you are reading after 31 December 2026, the claim window has closed and the paid-plan comparison below is the part that still applies.

Who qualifies for the free student year?

Google's conditions are about enrolment, not grades. You need to be currently enrolled at an eligible college or university in Pakistan — school pupils do not qualify — and you claim with a personal Google account. You must be 18 or older. How to claim it, step by step

The claim takes a few minutes and happens entirely on Google's own site. Do not use any other link, and never pay anyone to 'activate' the offer — Google charges nothing to claim it, so anyone asking for a fee is running a scam.

If your bank card is declined when adding the payment method, that is a card-settings problem, not an eligibility problem — the troubleshooting in our guide on how to pay for ChatGPT Plus in Pakistan (enabling international transactions, the Section 236Y advance tax, and which local payment methods work where) applies to Google checkouts too.

  • Step 1: Go to gemini.google/students and sign in with your personal Google account.
  • Step 2: Choose the free student plan and complete student verification when Google asks for it.
  • Step 3: Add a valid payment method. No charge is taken for the free year, but Google will not start the plan without one.
  • Step 4: Confirm the plan shows as Google AI Plus with a 12-month promotional period before you finish.
  • Step 5: Immediately set a phone reminder for month 11 to decide whether to cancel — the plan renews automatically (next sections).

The catch: it renews at about Rs 1,400 a month

The free year is genuine, but it is not free forever. Google's offer terms state that unless you cancel earlier, the plan automatically converts to a paid subscription when the 12 months end, charging the payment method you added at sign-up — in Pakistan, about Rs 1,400 per month at the current price. Students who claim in December 2026 and forget will start paying around December 2027.

Cancelling is done in your Google account's subscriptions, takes a minute, and you keep the plan until the end of the free period either way — so the safe routine is: claim now, set the month-11 reminder today, and decide with a clear head later. If you cancel, your storage falls back toward the free 15 GB, so download or clear anything above that before the plan ends; your files are not deleted overnight, but a full account stops syncing new content.

What happens after 31 December 2026?

The deadline is for claiming, not for using: anyone who redeems by 31 December 2026 keeps the full 12 free months from their claim date. After the deadline, Google has not announced an extension, so treat the date as final.

When your own free year runs out, you have three honest options: let it renew at about Rs 1,400/month, cancel and drop back to free Gemini, or move to a PKR plan such as the ones in the table above. Questions before you choose

Is Google AI Plus really free for Pakistani students?

Yes — for eligible students. If you are 18 or older, currently enrolled at a college or university in Pakistan and pass Google's student verification, you get 12 months of Google AI Plus at no cost when you claim at gemini.google/students by 31 December 2026. A valid payment method is required at sign-up, and the plan renews at about Rs 1,400/month after the free year unless you cancel.

Is Google AI Pro free in Pakistan too?

No. The free student year of Google AI Pro is for students in the United States only. In Pakistan, the free student plan is Google AI Plus — a lower tier than Pro, with 400 GB of storage and higher limits than a free Gemini account.

Why does Google ask for a payment method if the plan is free?

Because the plan converts to a paid subscription automatically when the 12 free months end. Google takes no charge during the free year; the payment method is there so renewal can happen without interruption. Set a reminder for month 11 and cancel in your Google account subscriptions if you do not want to pay.

What if I am not a student?

You cannot claim this offer — it is verified against current enrolment. Your options are the free Gemini tier, or a paid plan bought in PKR: AI Tools Zone lists Gemini Pro at PKR 2,000 for 1 year and ChatGPT Plus at PKR 3,000/month, payable via JazzCash or Easypaisa. See the pricing page for the current catalogue.

What happens to my 400 GB of storage and my files if I cancel?

When the plan ends, your storage falls back toward the free Google account limit of 15 GB. Files already stored are not deleted overnight, but an account over its limit stops syncing and adding new content — download or clear anything above 15 GB before the plan ends.

Can someone activate the student offer for me for a fee?

No — never pay for this. The offer is claimed only on Google's official student page, gemini.google/students, and Google charges nothing to claim it. Anyone asking for payment to 'activate' the free student year is running a scam.
