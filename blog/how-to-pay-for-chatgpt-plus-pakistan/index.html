Why local bank cards decline and how to troubleshoot

When attempting to upgrade to ChatGPT Plus from Pakistan, debit and credit cards frequently decline at web checkout. This does not indicate an account ban; rather, it typically reflects banking controls, card permissions, or settlement requirements applied by Pakistani financial institutions (such as Meezan Bank, HBL, Bank Alfalah, MCB, UBL, or Standard Chartered) on foreign recurring subscriptions. Cardholders should confirm international online transaction permissions directly with their issuing bank.

Three common factors trigger transaction failures at web checkout: first, international e-commerce sessions are disabled by default on many local payment cards until explicitly activated via mobile banking or phone helpline; second, 3D Secure SMS one-time password (OTP) delivery can experience cross-border telecom routing delays or timeouts; and third, unexpected fee buffers. Under Section 236Y of the Income Tax Ordinance, commercial banks collect an advance income tax on qualifying foreign payments remitted abroad via credit, debit, or prepaid cards on behalf of the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). According to the official FBR Tax Year 2027 Withholding Tax Rate Card (page 17, updated to June 30, 2026 under the Finance Act 2026), the advance tax rate under Section 236Y is 0.5% for individuals on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL) and 1.0% for non-ATL persons. This is an FBR advance withholding tax collected by commercial banks at remittance, not a State Bank of Pakistan tax.

Separately from government advance withholding tax, commercial banks and card networks apply their own foreign exchange conversion markups (typically 3%–5%, though spreads vary across banks, card networks, and interbank conditions) plus standard international transaction processing fees. If an account does not hold a sufficient buffer to cover both the base USD subscription price, bank conversion charges, and the statutory Section 236Y advance tax, the checkout transaction will decline.

Tax laws, withholding rates, and bank fee schedules are subject to change by regulators and financial institutions. Readers should verify current rates directly with the FBR and their card-issuing bank. This guide is provided for educational purposes only and does not constitute personal tax, legal, or financial advice.

  • Open your mobile banking app or call your bank's helpline to verify whether international e-commerce permissions are enabled on your card.
  • Maintain a sufficient balance buffer above the spot exchange rate (budgeting approximately 5%–8% extra depending on your issuing bank's FX conversion spread, card network assessment, and the 0.5% ATL or 1.0% non-ATL advance withholding tax under Section 236Y).
  • Confirm whether your bank requires a temporary session activation window before you initiate an international online transaction.

Official payment options: Web checkout and mobile app stores

OpenAI supports two primary direct payment channels: web checkout via Stripe (accepting Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards) and in-app billing through official ChatGPT mobile applications on the Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play Store (Android).

When web checkout declines due to direct merchant gateway restrictions, some Pakistani subscribers report success upgrading through the mobile app stores. Because Google Play and Apple process payment settlement through their platform merchant infrastructure, certain local cards that restrict recurring web Stripe transactions may process in-app charges. However, acceptance remains dependent on individual bank policies, card tiers, and account region settings—not all Pakistani financial institutions support recurring in-app mobile subscriptions.

  • Check whether your debit or credit card can be linked to your Google Play or Apple App Store account to attempt an in-app upgrade.
  • Confirm which Apple ID or Google account manages the subscription to track monthly renewals, cancellations, and receipts.
  • Avoid repeatedly resubmitting a declined web transaction, as rapid successive attempts may cause payment gateways or card issuers to place temporary fraud prevention holds on your card.
