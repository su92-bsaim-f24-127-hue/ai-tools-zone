Start with the official payment route

To subscribe directly, sign in to ChatGPT and use the upgrade flow shown in your account. OpenAI's accepted payment methods and plan availability can change; check its current billing help for your country and checkout. ChatGPT subscriptions may also be managed through supported mobile app stores.

A declined payment does not identify one universal cause. Check the message shown by checkout, verify payment details with your issuer or app-store provider, and ask whether the transaction is supported.   • Check the payment methods currently listed by OpenAI for your location.

Use a mobile app subscription only after checking billing

OpenAI says ChatGPT subscriptions may be billed through the web, Apple App Store or Google Play Store. The available route and payment methods can vary. Before starting, check which account and platform will manage the subscription so you can find renewal, cancellation and receipts later.

  • Use the official ChatGPT app and confirm the subscription inside the app-store billing flow.
  • Check which Apple or Google account and payment method will be charged.
  • Avoid starting a second subscription on another platform until you have checked for an existing one.

If you are considering a third-party seller listing

A seller listing is separate from a subscription bought directly from OpenAI. Before sending money, confirm the exact plan, account owner, access method, duration, renewal, support and refund terms in writing. OpenAI says an account is meant for the individual who created it; a seller's label such as Private does not by itself establish who created or controls the account.

Do not share your email password, OpenAI password, one-time code or recovery code with a seller. If you want an account that you personally control, ask the seller to explain the setup before paying and compare it with subscribing directly through OpenAI.

  • Read the listing's access type and duration, then ask for any unclear conditions.
  • Confirm final price, delivery estimate, payment options and written support/refund terms directly with the seller.
  • Check the provider's current account and subscription policies before choosing an access arrangement.

Keep your account and payment secure

Use a strong, unique password and account security settings. Never send passwords, one-time codes, payment-card details or recovery codes in a WhatsApp chat. If you already paid for a seller offer, keep your receipt and written terms and contact official OpenAI support for questions about the account itself.
