Why do Pakistani bank cards decline on ElevenLabs?

Paying in PKR through local rails avoids the decline risk and the foreign-transaction charges entirely:

  • International and recurring transactions are restricted by default: many local debit cards need online and international use explicitly enabled, and recurring foreign subscriptions are still frequently declined.
  • 3D Secure friction: foreign checkouts do not always complete the SMS OTP step Pakistani cards require.
  • Bank FX margin and fees: your bank converts above the interbank rate and adds foreign-transaction charges, so the debit exceeds the converted dollar price.
  • FBR Section 236Y advance tax: for Tax Year 2027 (from 1 July 2026) the FBR rate is 0.5% if you are on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% if you are not, adjustable when you file. Ignore guides quoting 10% or 16% — those figures are invented.
