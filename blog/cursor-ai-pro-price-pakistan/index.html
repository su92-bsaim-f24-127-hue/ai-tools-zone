What changed in Cursor's pricing in October 2026?

Cursor replaced its plan lineup on 12 October 2026 as part of The X Subscription, one subscription covering Grok, Cursor and X. The old Hobby, Pro ($20) and Pro+ ($60) plans still work for existing subscribers, but new customers cannot buy them, and switching away from a legacy plan is permanent.

  • The entry price for Claude inside Cursor rose: the old Pro included an allowance for third-party models, while on the new plans the included usage pool covers Cursor's own models (Grok and Composer). Claude, GPT and Gemini are billed separately at API list prices from prepaid credit, and only from the $30 Premium tier up — the $8 Plus plan cannot use them at all.
  • There is no longer a 500 fast requests quota to compare: usage is now a pooled allowance, so any guide still quoting 500 fast requests and unlimited slow requests is describing the discontinued plan.

Why Pakistani bank cards get declined on Cursor

Pakistani developers commonly hit three problems when paying Cursor directly:

  • International transactions are off or limited by default: many local debit cards need international and online transactions explicitly enabled, and recurring foreign subscriptions are frequently declined even then.
  • Bank foreign-exchange margins and fees: your bank converts at its own rate, not the interbank rate, and adds its foreign-transaction charges, so the final debit is higher than the dollar price converted at 276.61.
  • FBR Section 236Y advance tax: banks collect advance tax on card payments abroad. For Tax Year 2027 (from 1 July 2026) the FBR rate is 0.5% if you are on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% if you are not, and it is adjustable against your income tax when you file. Be wary of guides quoting 5%, 10% or higher — those are last year's rates or simply wrong.
