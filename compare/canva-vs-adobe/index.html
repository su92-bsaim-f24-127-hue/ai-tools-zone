Start with the handover format

Ask whether the recipient needs a finished image, a shareable design or an editable file in a particular application. Try opening and editing a representative sample before choosing a subscription. This is more useful than comparing broad product labels.

Inspect the exact access offer

The Canva listing is marked Pro Edu and Invitation; the Adobe listing is marked Private. Confirm education eligibility, workspace restrictions and the Adobe applications included. These are not equivalent retail plans with identical conditions.

Check assets and collaboration

Review rights for fonts, templates and stock elements used in client work. Confirm how collaborators receive access and whether exports remain usable when a plan ends. We do not rate either vendor on benchmark speed or output quality.

Questions before you choose

Which should a beginner choose?

Try the workflow that produces your required deliverable with the least rework. If an editable Adobe file is mandatory, a visually similar export from another tool may not satisfy the brief.

Does the Canva listing prove education eligibility?

No. Check Canva's own education requirements and ask exactly how the invitation is supplied before ordering.
