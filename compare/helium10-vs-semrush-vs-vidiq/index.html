These tools do different jobs

The vendors describe distinct product areas: marketplace seller operations, SEO and search marketing, and YouTube creator workflows. Treat this as a channel-selection guide, not a head-to-head performance test. If your work spans more than one channel, check whether a second paid subscription is actually needed.

Use a matching pilot

Choose one task your business already performs. For e-commerce research, verify the marketplace and the product data you need. For search marketing, use your target site and country. For YouTube, test a real channel and video topic. Record whether the data changes a decision you would otherwise make.
