Separate editing from generation

Write down whether your project starts with footage or needs newly generated media. An editing workflow and a generative workflow have different inputs, iteration costs and review needs; one product label does not establish that either replaces the other.

Run a small production test

Use a short, non-client sample with the same target aspect ratio and delivery format. Track setup time, usable outputs, corrections, export quality and any watermark. For generative clips, include discarded attempts when estimating cost.

Check rights, credits and current access

Read the provider's current pricing and terms for export limits, model access, commercial use and credit behavior. AI Tools Zone currently lists a CapCut offer, but not Runway; the local listing's access and duration are separate from CapCut's official plan benefits.

Questions before you choose

Is CapCut the same kind of tool as Runway?

They can serve different stages of a video workflow. Compare them using the source material and final deliverable your project requires.

Does AI Tools Zone offer Runway?

Runway is not currently listed. The CapCut page describes only the seller-listed CapCut offer; it does not promise Runway access.

Can I estimate generative-video cost from a monthly credit count alone?

Not reliably. Model, duration, resolution and failed attempts can affect usage. Check the provider's current credit rules and test your own short project.
