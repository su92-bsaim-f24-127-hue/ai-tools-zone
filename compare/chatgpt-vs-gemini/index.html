ChatGPT vs Gemini: PKR Plans & Access

Both ChatGPT and Gemini are general AI assistants. In this catalog, the important purchase difference is the specific offer: compare the listed duration and Private versus Invitation access before deciding from the headline price.

Compare with the same task

Give both assistants the same brief, input and requested output. Check whether they follow constraints and whether their reasoning or references can be verified. We have not run a controlled performance benchmark and do not declare an overall winner.

Price is not an equivalent-plan benchmark

The offers below have different access arrangements and durations. The figures are seller-listed PKR amounts, not official vendor prices or proof of equal entitlements. Ask which current plan, models, tools and limits you will actually receive.

How to make the choice

Prefer the option that completes your repeated tasks with acceptable review effort and account conditions. If either vendor offers a suitable free option, test your workflow before paying. For confidential documents, understand the account and workspace controls first.
