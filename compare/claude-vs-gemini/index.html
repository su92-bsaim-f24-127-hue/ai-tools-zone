Compare workflow fit, not brand claims

Use one representative task from your week: summarize a document, draft a reply, or organize research. Check whether the answer is accurate, whether you can trace its sources and what steps remain outside the assistant. A vendor's feature list is not a side-by-side quality benchmark.

Treat plan and region details separately

Google AI membership benefits can vary by plan and product availability. Anthropic describes its own Claude plans separately from API usage. Confirm country, account, usage limits and billing terms directly with each provider. The current AI Tools Zone catalogue contains a Gemini seller-listed offer; it does not contain Claude.

Keep your data and cancellation needs in view

Before using either service for work, review data controls, workspace ownership and export options. Check renewal and cancellation terms on the provider's own page. Avoid choosing from a headline price if access, duration or eligibility differ.
