## 1. Internal Linking Architecture & SEO Equity Distribution

Internal linking connects transactional product listings, comparison research pages, editorial guides, and customer support hubs into a coherent semantic web. 

This linking framework fulfills three core objectives:
1. **Pass PageRank & Link Equity:** Channelling authority from high-traffic guides and comparisons directly to catalog product offer pages.
2. **Eliminate Orphan Pages & Reduce Crawl Depth:** Ensuring all 83 indexable pages (including the new `/faq/` hub) are reachable within 1 to 2 clicks from the homepage.
3. **Enhance User Journey & WhatsApp Conversion:** Directing search visitors reading an FAQ or comparison directly to the relevant product page or pre-formatted WhatsApp order link.
