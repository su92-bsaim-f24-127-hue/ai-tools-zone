# AI Tools Pak competitor review

Research date: 22 October 2026. Website reviewed: [aitoolspak.tech](https://aitoolspak.tech/).

## Scope and evidence

This was a public, read-only review. I checked the homepage, `robots.txt`, the complete XML sitemap, the published `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` and `agents.md`; I also inspected representative storefront, price-index, long-directory, product-detail, comparison, editorial, social-services and API-offer pages. The sitemap contains 233 URLs: 115 under `/blog/`, 34 under `/ai-post/`, nine under `/ai-income-lab/`, eight under `/comparisons/`, and the remainder are product, policy, service, discovery and machine-readable pages. These counts describe the publisher's sitemap, not the number of pages that Google indexed or the quality, traffic or search rank of those pages.

The site's published robots file permits public crawling and lists its sitemap. Its AI-readable catalogue files are directly accessible; `llms-full.txt` is a large, roughly 0.5 MB document. These files describe the publisher's own business and offers. They do not establish independent verification, Google rankings or a special ranking benefit from the file format.

## Useful caution from the price-index review

The competitor's index is explicitly described as the publisher's own seller-price snapshot, not official vendor pricing or a universal market average. One visible listing also differs between pages fetched during this review: the price index lists ChatGPT Plus at PKR 1,833, while the homepage and its product page list PKR 2,200. The index carries a stated 19 August 2026 update date and the product page carries an August 2026 price-check date. This observation does not establish which figure is correct; it demonstrates why AI Tools Zone should source its own page from its own catalog and avoid copying competitor figures or treating a label as independent verification.

Similarly, a product-source link can verify the identity of a vendor or plan without proving that a separate seller's price, delivery, access rights or warranty are accurate. Keep those facts distinct in the customer journey.

## Changes made to AI Tools Zone

1. Added **PKR prices** to the homepage's main navigation, linking to the existing owner-catalog-driven `/pricing/` page.
2. Added a no-match WhatsApp request to catalogue search. The customer's typed term is escaped in the visible page, URL-encoded in the draft, and capped at 120 characters. The customer must review and send the message; no order or claim is made automatically.
3. Added a browser check that verifies the unlisted-search request contains the searched name.
4. Added **Compare**, **Blog**, and **AI for Business** to the homepage and generated-page navigation, linking to the stable `/compare/` hub, the `/blog/` hub, and the business automation guide. The comparison hub H1 matches “Compare AI Tools in Pakistan”; existing comparison routes remain in place.
5. Added source-linked references to Meta's current WhatsApp Business policy/pricing pages and Twilio's voice-agent/human-handoff documentation. The page gives evaluation and safety guidance only; it does not claim that AI Tools Zone deploys bots or sells the competitor's services.

No competitor article text, product offer/price, discount, rating, customer quote, logo or service capability was imported. The new route is an educational guide, not a custom-service order page. These changes improve discovery and topical coverage; they do not prove a future ranking, traffic or conversion increase.

## Full sitemap content follow-up — 22 October 2026

A fresh read-only fetch of the competitor sitemap returned 233 URLs. Of these, 115 are under `/blog/` (the hub plus 114 article URLs), and eight are under `/comparisons/` (the comparison hub plus seven detail pages). The public comparison hub links seven topics. The blog covers a wide mix of recurring buyer questions and fast-moving news; the visible news index carried August 2026 publication dates in the October 2026 review, so those stories were not imported as current advice. The sitemap and hub are treated as an inventory, not a request to duplicate the publisher's article bodies.

AI Tools Zone now covers all seven comparison-hub subjects through original routes: ChatGPT/Claude, ChatGPT/Gemini, Claude/Gemini, writing assistants, CapCut/Runway, ElevenLabs/PlayHT, and e-commerce/SEO/YouTube research tools. Six comparison routes were added; the existing ChatGPT/Gemini route was retained. Each page uses official vendor references and a task-specific test checklist. Its table shows current local offer links only for products in AI Tools Zone's own catalogue; unlisted products are labeled as not listed and have no invented PKR price.

Five additional original blog guides were added for free-first AI selection, freelancers, teachers, content creators and the difference between an app subscription and API credits. Existing pages already cover other overlapping competitor topics such as student, developer, business, pricing, safe-buying and product decisions, so these were linked rather than cloned into duplicate URLs. The complete local sitemap is now 66 URLs and the keyword map has 248 bounded mappings. Commit `14620f2` is live through the primary GitHub Pages repository. The production crawl passed with 66 sitemap URLs; Search Console indexing remains unverified. The push to the second GitHub repository was rejected with HTTP 403 because the authenticated account lacks write access.
