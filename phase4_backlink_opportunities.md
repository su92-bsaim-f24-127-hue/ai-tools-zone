## 1. Backlink Acquisition Philosophy

For a localized e-commerce marketplace and software comparison engine in Pakistan, backlink quality and topical relevance vastly outperform raw link volume. Google and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) evaluate:
1. **Entity Citations & Consistency:** Consistent Name, Address, Phone, Domain (NAPD) across tech directories.
2. **Local Geo-Relevance:** Inbound links from Pakistani tech news sites (`.pk` domains, ProPakistani, TechJuice, Daily Times).
3. **Topical Authority:** Inclusion in curated global AI and SaaS discovery hubs (Futurepedia, Toolify, There's An AI For That).
4. **Editorial Citations:** Mentions in freelancer guides, university blogs, and developer documentation as a trusted local payment solution.
