# AI Tools Zone: earned-link plan

Prepared 6 October 2026. This is a practical, low-risk plan for earning relevant references to `https://aitoolszone.tech/`. It does not predict rankings, traffic or customers, and no outreach has been sent.

## Position worth citing

AI Tools Zone is an independent Pakistan-focused catalog of seller-listed AI and digital subscription offers. Its defensible contribution is making each listing easier to evaluate: PKR total, duration, access type, limitations, and a way to confirm details with the seller. Do not imply official vendor authorization, independently tested product quality, guaranteed availability, or a verified lowest price unless evidence supports that exact claim.

## Make useful reference material first

Prioritize a small set of pages that help an author answer a real reader question:

1. Keep the [catalog methodology guide](https://aitoolszone.tech/guides/catalog-methodology/) clear about what prices and access labels mean, how offers are compared, and what has not been independently verified.
2. Improve the [private versus shared access guide](https://aitoolszone.tech/guides/private-vs-shared-access/) with plain-language buyer checks and links to authoritative vendor account/security guidance. Do not frame shared access as equivalent to an individual vendor subscription.
3. Maintain the student, creator and developer buying guides from current catalog facts. Show the date and calculation method for any price comparison; avoid fabricated savings, review scores or recommendations presented as hands-on testing.
4. If enough dated observations accumulate, publish an original Pakistan-focused price/access snapshot with a reproducible method and archived source notes. Do not invent historical prices or turn a single snapshot into a market-wide survey.

Each asset should stand on its own for a visitor who never clicks an order button. Add a contextual internal link to the relevant product or guide, but do not make external publishers use a required anchor phrase.

## Relevant places to earn mentions

Build a short, human-reviewed prospect list from:

- Pakistani technology, education and startup publications that actually cover software buying or digital skills.
- University technology societies, coding communities and credible training organizations where a guide would help their learners.
- Local founder, developer and creator communities that accept useful resources in their normal editorial process.
- Vendor/community documentation only when the page genuinely helps users understand account type, eligibility or security; do not ask a vendor to endorse a third-party resale offer.
- Genuine business profiles and directories that serve real customers. Keep name, website and contact details consistent; never create a fictitious address or duplicate profile.

For each prospect, record its audience, the exact page that would help, the editor/contact route published by that organization, the date checked, and the outcome. Skip sites that only sell ranking links, publish unrelated guest posts, or have no real audience fit. A relevant unlinked brand mention or a referral visit can be useful even without a followed link.

## A modest 90-day sequence

**Weeks 1–2:** Review the reference pages for accuracy, working URLs, visible source attribution and mobile readability. Confirm all offer facts with the supplier before presenting them as current. Prepare one concise explanation of the methodology guide and one of the access guide.

**Weeks 3–6:** Identify up to 10 relevant publications or communities manually. Read their contribution rules and recent coverage. Contact only the editor or public submission route that fits. Make a specific suggestion tied to an existing article or a clearly useful resource; offer facts and a source link, and leave editorial control with them.

**Weeks 7–10:** Improve the material based on real questions received. Share it in communities only where self-promotion is allowed and disclose the business relationship. Participate to answer the question, not to drop a link.

**Weeks 11–13:** Review earned referring domains and referring pages in Search Console/Bing, aggregate referrals in the available analytics dashboard, and organic impressions/clicks for the referenced guides. Keep only outreach approaches that brought relevant citations, readers or genuine feedback. Review again after another 90 days rather than judging a few weeks as a ranking result.

Suggested one-to-one note (adapt only when the recipient's work is genuinely relevant):

> Assalam-o-alaikum, I maintain AI Tools Zone, an independent Pakistan-focused catalog of seller-listed digital subscriptions. Your [specific article/resource] explains [specific reader need]. We have a plain-language guide to [relevant access/pricing question], including what our listings do and do not verify: [one relevant URL]. If it helps your readers, you are welcome to reference it; no link text or endorsement is requested. If it is not a fit, no reply is needed.

Do not send bulk or automated messages. Do not claim a relationship with the recipient or vendor that does not exist.

## Measure outcomes that matter

- Earned referring sites and pages: check Search Console's Links report and Bing Webmaster Tools. Review the source page itself for topical relevance and whether the link is editorially useful.
- Referral quality: use aggregate referrer data available in Cloudflare Web Analytics. A visit or WhatsApp click is not a confirmed lead or sale.
- Search visibility: compare equivalent date windows for impressions and clicks to the destination guides in Search Console. Position fluctuates by query, locale, device and time; do not treat a single manual search as a rank report.
- Business outcome: the owner may privately count qualified inquiries and completed orders. Do not upload customer names, phone numbers or message text into a backlink spreadsheet or public repository.

Do not optimize for third-party domain-authority scores as if they were Google metrics. Do not set a target number of links; relevance and usefulness matter more than raw count.

## Avoid link schemes

Do not buy links or “guest posts” intended to pass ranking credit, trade excessive reciprocal links, use private blog networks, automated directory submissions, comment/forum drops, keyword-stuffed anchors, distributed footer links, or rewards in exchange for followed links. If a genuine sponsorship or paid placement is appropriate for audience reach, clearly disclose it and ensure the publisher qualifies the link with `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`. Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) identify ranking-focused paid links, automated link creation, low-quality directories and excessive exchanges as link spam examples.

1. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools ownership, then submit the sitemap. See [Search Console setup](SEARCH-CONSOLE-SETUP.md) and [Bing setup](BING-WEBMASTER-SETUP.md).
2. Confirm the current seller terms and each public claim before asking another site to cite a guide.
3. Choose whether to run the small outreach sequence. The prospect list and notes should remain private; only publish facts and materials suitable for every visitor.
