Account Ownership & Access Models: Private, Invitation, Shared & Keys

Not all software subscriptions work the same way. Before placing an order, confirm which access type matches your privacy and workflow requirements:

  • Private Account: Dedicated login credentials (email and password) issued exclusively to you. Your chat history, saved files, and custom preferences remain 100% confidential. Recommended for proprietary software engineering, confidential client deliverables, and private academic research.
  • Invitation Access: An official workspace or team invitation sent directly to your existing personal email address (e.g. Canva Pro Edu or Notion Business). You retain your personal login, and all work is stored in your private seat.
  • Shared Profile: A budget-conscious option where multiple users share login access to a single account with assigned profile slots. Best suited for testing new tools or non-confidential learning exercises.
  • License Key: Genuine retail digital product keys for direct software activation (e.g. Windows 11 Pro). Provides permanent, genuine machine-level activation verified by vendor servers.
