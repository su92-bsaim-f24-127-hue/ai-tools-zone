### Q1.3: Why do Pakistani bank debit/credit cards fail on international AI websites?
**A:** Most Pakistani commercial banks restrict international e-commerce by default to protect against card fraud. Furthermore, foreign transactions are subject to:
1. Advance income tax on foreign transactions (10% for active tax filers, up to 16% for non-filers).
2. Bank foreign exchange markups (typically 3%–5% above interbank rates).
3. Two-factor authentication (OTP) delivery delays.
By purchasing through AI Tools Zone, you pay in flat Pakistani Rupees (PKR) with zero foreign transaction markups or unexpected tax deductions.

## Category 2: Access Types & Account Delivery Mechanics

### Q2.1: What does "Private" access mean?
**A:** A **Private** account means you receive personal, dedicated access:
- You log in using your own email or dedicated credentials provided to you.
- Your chat histories, saved templates, bookmarks, and project files are 100% private and visible only to you.
- No other customer has access to your workspace.
- Examples in our catalog: *Gemini Pro (1 Year)*, *ChatGPT Plus (1 Month Private)*, *Figma Pro (1 Year)*.

### Q2.2: What does "Shared" access mean, and what are the rules?
**A:** A **Shared** account provides access to a premium subscription where the subscription cost is split among a limited group of users to make the software affordable:
- You receive verified login credentials for an active premium account.
- **Usage Rules:** You must not change the account password, email, or profile settings.
- Shared accounts are ideal for individual freelancers and students looking to access expensive tools on a budget.
- Examples in our catalog: *CapCut Pro (PKR 900)*, *Leonardo AI Essential (PKR 1,500)*, *Adobe Creative Cloud (PKR 3,500)*.

### Q2.3: What does "Invitation" access mean?
**A:** An **Invitation** plan means premium access is delivered directly to your own personal email address via a team, workspace, or family invitation:
- You do not share login passwords with anyone.
- You accept the invitation sent to your email, and your existing account is instantly upgraded to premium.
- Examples in our catalog: *Canva Pro Edu (Lifetime Invite)*, *YouTube Premium (1 Month Invite)*.

### Q2.4: What is a "License Key"?
**A:** A **License Key** is an official retail digital activation code that permanently activates software on your device:
- The key is delivered directly in WhatsApp chat with official Microsoft download links.
- Example in our catalog: *Windows 11 Pro Key (PKR 1,500 Lifetime Activation)*.
