What does Google NotebookLM Pro do?

Google NotebookLM is the groundbreaking personalized AI research assistant grounded strictly in your own uploaded documents. Powered by Gemini 1.5 Pro, NotebookLM eliminates hallucinations by answering queries exclusively using your PDFs, Google Docs, and slides, and features the viral 'Audio Overview' tool that generates two-host conversational podcast deep dives from your notes.

  • Turn a 200-page academic thesis or textbook into an entertaining 15-minute podcast episode
  • Ask complex cross-source questions across dozens of company policy documents
  • Prepare comprehensive exam study guides and flashcards in seconds

Main features and capabilities

  • Strict source grounding: answers cite exact page numbers and quotes from your documents
  • Audio Overviews: generates engaging, two-host conversational podcasts from your notes
  • Supports uploads of up to 50 sources per notebook (PDFs, Google Docs, web links, audio)
  • Automated study guide, briefing document, timeline, and FAQ generation
  • Private and secure: your personal uploaded notes are never used to train public Google models

Key benefits for Pakistani users

  • Zero hallucinations: every answer includes clickable citations to the exact source page
  • The ultimate study and review tool for Pakistani university and medical students
  • Assisted setup on your private Google account

Frequently Asked Questions about Google NotebookLM Pro

What is an Audio Overview in NotebookLM?

Audio Overview uses AI to generate a realistic, banter-filled conversational podcast between two AI hosts who explain and discuss the contents of your uploaded documents.

Will NotebookLM hallucinate information from the internet?

No. NotebookLM is strictly grounded in your uploaded source materials and will explicitly state if an answer is not contained in your notes.
