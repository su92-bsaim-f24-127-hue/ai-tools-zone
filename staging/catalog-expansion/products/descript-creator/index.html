Frequently Asked Questions about Descript Creator

How does text-based video editing work in Descript?

Descript transcribes your audio into text. When you highlight and delete a word or sentence in the text, Descript automatically trims that exact section from your video.

What is Studio Sound in Descript?

Studio Sound uses AI to isolate your voice, remove room echo, and eliminate background noise, making your audio sound like it was recorded in a professional soundproof studio.
