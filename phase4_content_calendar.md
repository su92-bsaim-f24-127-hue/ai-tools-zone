## 1. Publishing Rules & Anti-Cannibalization Architecture

To avoid keyword cannibalization and thin content:
1. **One Primary Intent per URL:** Never publish two articles competing for the same root transactional keyword.
2. **Strict Spoke-to-Pillar Internal Linking:** Every blog guide must link upward to its parent Category Hub (`/categories/*`), its commercial Product Offer (`/products/*`), and its Head-to-Head Comparison page (`/compare/*`).
3. **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** Distinguish hands-on workflow evaluation tests from vendor marketing claims.
4. **Local Commercial Hooks:** Every piece must include Pakistan-specific context (PKR costs, JazzCash/EasyPaisa payment options, bank forex card decline solutions).
