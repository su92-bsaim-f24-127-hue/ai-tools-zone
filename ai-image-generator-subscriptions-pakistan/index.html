Understanding Credit Systems, Token Economics & Speed Queues

Unlike unlimited chatbots, AI image generation platforms operate on token or credit systems due to heavy GPU computational demands:

Fast vs. Relaxed Tokens: Fast tokens prioritize your prompt on cloud GPU clusters, rendering high-definition visuals in 5 to 10 seconds. Leonardo AI Essential grants 8,500 fast tokens monthly.

Resolution Costs: Standard 512×512 images cost 1 token, while upscaled 4K photorealistic images using Alchemy v2 require 8 to 14 tokens.

Free vs. Paid Privacy: Free tier generations on platforms like Leonardo or Midjourney are publicly visible in community galleries. Paid subscriptions ensure prompt and image privacy.
