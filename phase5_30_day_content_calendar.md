## 1. Editorial Architecture & Quality Standards

Every article in this calendar adheres to five strict publishing criteria:
1. **Topical Hub-and-Spoke Alignment:** Every article acts as an educational spoke designed to pass internal link equity to a primary Product Page (`/products/*`), a Category Hub (`/categories/*`), and a Comparison Hub (`/compare/*`).
2. **Distinct Search Intent:** No proposed article competes with any existing canonical route on `aitoolszone.tech`.
3. **Hyper-Localized Practicality:** Each guide addresses concrete Pakistani realities—such as SBP advance withholding taxes, bank debit card blocks, JazzCash/EasyPaisa payments, Urdu language nuances, and electricity/load-shedding workflow resilience.
4. **Actionable Conversion Bridges:** Natural, non-intrusive calls to action connecting the educational topic to direct WhatsApp order links with verified PKR prices.
5. **No AI Padding:** Every article specifies concrete step-by-step workflows, tool settings, and pricing calculations rather than vague generalities.
