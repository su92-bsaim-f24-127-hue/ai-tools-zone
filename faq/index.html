Why do local Pakistani debit cards get declined on official AI platforms?

+

Most Pakistani commercial bank cards decline international software subscriptions (such as OpenAI, Adobe, Midjourney, or Claude) due to State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) foreign exchange regulations, mandatory 3D-Secure OTP mismatches on recurring subscriptions, international transaction withholding taxes (often adding 10%–16% extra charges), or default international eCommerce restrictions on standard debit cards. AI Tools Zone solves this payment friction by accepting domestic PKR payments without international card requirements. Read our ChatGPT Plus Pakistan payment guide.

D. Account Access and Security

Understanding Private, Shared, Invitation, and License Key subscriptions.

What is the difference between Private, Shared, and Invitation access?

+

Private means you receive dedicated account credentials used exclusively by you. Invitation means an official invite is sent to your email to join a managed workspace while keeping your own password. Shared means multiple verified users share access to a single master account with dedicated profiles (like Netflix Premium 4K or Surfshark VPN). Review our detailed Private vs Shared Access Guide before purchasing.

Can other users see my private documents, chats, or projects on a Shared plan?

+

On shared accounts with individual user profiles (like streaming or VPN services), profiles are distinct. However, for AI chat assistants (ChatGPT Plus or Gemini Pro), AI Tools Zone lists Private access so that your prompt history, uploaded PDF files, and client deliverables remain strictly private to you.

How can I avoid account lockouts or bans on shared subscriptions?

+

To ensure uninterrupted service on shared plans: 1) Never change the master account email, password, or billing settings; 2) Only stream or log in on your assigned screen/profile; 3) Avoid logging into an excessive number of simultaneous devices. Learn more in our guide on avoiding account bans on shared digital subscriptions.

Does AI Tools Zone support two-factor authentication (2FA)?

+

On dedicated Private subscriptions (such as private developer or design accounts), you can enable your own authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) or security keys to secure your login credentials completely.
