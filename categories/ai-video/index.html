How to choose for your work

For footage you already have, start by testing your editing workflow. For a missing visual, evaluate generation separately and budget for revisions. Confirm export quality and permitted commercial use instead of treating the word Unlimited as a verified quota.

Questions before you choose

Which tool should I use for video editing?

Start with the CapCut listing when you need to edit existing clips. Check your device and the required export features before choosing the plan.

Does a video generation plan replace an editor?

Not necessarily. You may still need to assemble clips, adjust timing, add sound and review the final export in an editor.
