How to choose for your work

Shortlist Canva for layout work, Leonardo for image-generation exploration, Figma for interface work, or Adobe for the specific creative application you need. Confirm that the seller offer includes that application, export format and usage allowance.

Questions before you choose

Is Canva Pro Edu the same as a regular Canva Pro subscription?

Do not assume equivalence from the listing name. This catalog marks the offer as Invitation. Ask which workspace, eligibility and feature conditions apply, and check Canva's education information.

Can I use generated images in client work?

Check the relevant vendor's current licensing terms and the rights to your inputs. The marketplace listing alone is not a commercial-use license.
