How to choose for your work

Estimate finished audio length and likely retakes. Confirm the current character or credit allowance, supported export format and commercial-use rights. If a voice resembles a real person, obtain the permissions required for your intended use.

Questions before you choose

Can I make Urdu narration with this offer?

Ask for confirmation of the available models, languages and a suitable sample. The listing does not establish a quality benchmark for Urdu or a guarantee for every voice.

What should a YouTuber check before buying?

Check the voice allowance, rights for published content, pronunciation quality and whether revisions consume the same allowance as the first generation.
