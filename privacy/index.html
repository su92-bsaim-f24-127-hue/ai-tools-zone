Your privacy matters.

Data on this website

Your chosen light or dark theme is saved in this browser’s local storage. Search, filters and comparisons run in your browser. This site does not collect passwords, payment-card details or account credentials through a form. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits, page views and page performance. Cloudflare describes this service as cookie-free and does not use local storage to track visitors. Analytics reports are accessed through our Cloudflare account and are not displayed publicly on this website. Learn about Cloudflare Web Analytics.

We also use Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and selected site interactions, including product views and order-button clicks. The Google tag is configured to send the page path without query parameters. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers and sessions and may process the page path, referrer, browser/device information, approximate location and interaction data. Our site events can include a product identifier, but not names, email addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp message text, on-site search terms or payment details. See Google Analytics data and privacy information. You can control or clear cookies in your browser.
