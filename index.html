How do I place an order?＋

Add tools to your bag and choose “Order on WhatsApp,” or use the WhatsApp button in any tool’s details. Review and send the pre-filled message. We confirm the order and payment method with you.

Are these private or shared accounts?＋

It depends on the selected product. Every listing shows Private, Shared, Invitation or License Key. Invitation plans provide workspace or team access. Check the details and confirm the access conditions with us before payment.

How quickly will I get access?＋

The catalog estimates 15–60 minutes for most tools and 1–2 hours for selected professional plans after payment confirmation. Your tool’s exact estimate is shown in its details; availability is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Which payment methods can I use?＋

JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer can be arranged on WhatsApp. We share the payment details after confirming your plan and availability. There is no automatic charge on this website.

What about warranty and refunds?＋

Each product lists its replacement warranty, typically 7 or 30 days. If the confirmed plan cannot be delivered, you can request a full refund. After activation, replacement coverage follows the stated warranty. Contact us with your order details for assistance.
